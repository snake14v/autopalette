QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our premium detailing and protection services.

A full-body Paint Protection Film (PPF) installation typically takes 3 to 5 days. This includes washing, decontamination, paint correction, film application, and curing time.

Our premium WaveX Graphene Ceramic Coating provides peak durability for 3 to 5 years with proper maintenance and annual inspections.

Yes, we offer both high-gloss and matte (stealth) PPF. Applying matte PPF over glossy paint instantly transforms the look while offering maximum protection.

Light to medium scratches and swirl marks can be fully removed. Deep scratches that have penetrated the clear coat may require touch-up paint or panel repainting at our Body Shop.
